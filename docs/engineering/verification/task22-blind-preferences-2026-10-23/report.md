## Enforcement and limits

Only actual completed local requests with matching input/output digests and generation settings can enter formal review. Two to eight reviewer-role credentials are frozen, excluding generator credentials and known generator user accounts; known reviewer owners must be distinct. Initial votes require the current reviewer role, even after a credential is promoted. Managers resolve disagreements explicitly after all assigned votes or abstentions are present. Conflicts do not block another assigned reviewer. Corrections append records and retain original choices.

Optional source binding permits only exact eligible training rows and excludes full validation/test/audit groups. Current evaluation prompts/groups, changed model/tokenizer/rubric pins, inaccessible sources, missing reviews, stale revisions and unresolved conflicts refuse review or publication. Completed formal batches finish their campaign. Ties, abstentions, rejected and skipped work never silently become chosen/rejected choices. Oversized/formatted exports preserve explicit CPU-compatibility limits without truncation. See the [protocol guide](../../blind-preference-review.md).

Blinding protects system metadata; original response text can self-identify a model, and earlier human exposure cannot be undone. Separate credentials alone cannot establish independently acting people. Actual customer/domain preference collection and model-quality qualification remain separate and on hold under the developer's instruction.
